How this was counted

A listing arrived when it was absent from one Overture release and listed in the next two; it left (is counted as gone) when it was listed for two releases and then absent for two. One-month appearances and the newest, not-yet-confirmed month () are never counted as change. Counts are listings, not verified openings or closures: every number here describes what Overture lists for Vietnam, not what opened or closed there. Rates compare net change with the first month and are not shown for fewer than 20 listings.
