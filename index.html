How this was counted

A place arrived when it was absent from one Overture release and listed in the next two; it is gone when it was listed for two releases and then absent for two. One-month appearances and the newest, not-yet-confirmed month () are never counted as change. Counts are listings, not verified openings or closures. Rates compare net change with the first month and are not shown for fewer than 20 places.
